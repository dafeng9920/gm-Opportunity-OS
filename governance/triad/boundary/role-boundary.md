# Role boundary v0.1

- Execution records the assigned admission artifact; it cannot approve an opportunity.
- Review assesses a formal Execution artifact; it cannot modify implementation or replace Execution.
- Compliance assesses a formal Review artifact; it cannot decide business direction or replace missing facts.
- Only Compliance can issue a gate decision after all three formal artifacts exist.

An overstepping artifact or an incomplete chain is invalid, not a shortcut.
